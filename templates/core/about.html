About MedSync

Scheduling shouldn't be the hardest part of healthcare.

MedSync gives doctors control of their calendar and gives patients a way to book that takes seconds, not phone calls.

What we're building

Most appointment booking still runs on phone tag. A patient calls during business hours, waits on hold, and takes whatever slot is left. A practice spends hours a week on the other end of that call.

MedSync replaces that with one shared source of truth. Doctors publish their real availability once; patients see only genuinely open slots and book directly. Both sides get the same clear record of what was booked, moved, or cancelled.

What we care about

  • Trust

    Clear records, confirmed changes, no surprises.

  • Speed

    A doctor should read their day at a glance.

  • Access

    Usable on any device, by anyone, at any hour.
